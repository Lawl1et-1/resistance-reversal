A synthetic-simulator study · in silico only

Reversing resistance in silico

A drug-resistant cell and a drug-sensitive one are two stable states of the same system. This work learns how perturbations move a cell between them, searches for interventions that would cross the boundary — and calibrates when the model should not be believed.

I
Plate I · the mechanism

Reversal is a basin crossing

The simulator is a toggle switch: two master regulators, each activating itself and repressing the other. That gives two stable states with a boundary between them. A perturbation does not nudge expression. It moves the boundary, and the cell falls into whichever basin it then finds itself in.

Below is that system, integrated live from the model's own equations. Drag the cell. Knock down a regulator and watch the separatrix move.

II
Plate II · finding one

Calibration holds — then fails at the boundary, all at once

The model carries conformal prediction intervals, which promise to contain the truth 90% of the time. On held-out perturbations they do, almost exactly: —.

Move to a cell context the model never saw and three things fail together: coverage drops to —, the uncertainty estimate becomes no better than a coin flip at flagging its own errors (AUROC —), and abstaining on the least-confident predictions stops helping.

This is what conformal theory predicts when its exchangeability assumption breaks, so it is a confirmation, not a discovery. What is striking is that all three safety properties fail at the same boundary: the uncertainty machinery gives no warning precisely where it stops working.

IV
Plate IV · finding three, preliminary

The flow model collapses where its twin does not

On unseen cell contexts the flow model scores —, against — for ridge regression. An otherwise identical model that skips the iterative integration step scores —.

The two differ only in whether prediction integrates an ODE across twenty steps. A supporting check — reducing the integration budget at inference on fixed weights — recovers the loss on the shifted split while leaving an unshifted control flat. That points to error compounding across integration under distribution shift.

We stop there and name no cause. The obvious explanation, an untrained context embedding, predicts that longer training changes nothing; longer training measurably hurt. Supporting evidence exists only at small scale on one seed, so this is reported as an observation, not a result.

VI
Plate VI · corrections

Claims that did not survive

Thirteen defects were found and recorded during the build. Four of them would have produced a convincing but false result. These are the claims that were written down and then withdrawn. They hang here rather than being deleted, because the corrections are the evidence that the surviving numbers were checked.

01
Deep models fail to generalise to held-out genes — every model scored 0.00. Withdrawn: the split was unsolvable in principle.

Upstream regulators were mutually independent, so co-expression carried no information about gene function (embedding to effect correlation −0.11). No model could have transferred. The simulator was rebuilt with pathway modules; the correlation became +0.93.

02
The flow model predicts the post-perturbation population well. Withdrawn: it was collapsing the population to a point.

Conditioning the velocity field on the starting cell meant a deterministic ODE could only run to the conditional mean. Predicted spread 0.30 against a true 0.86, and an energy distance worse than predicting no change at all. Point metrics looked fine throughout.

03
The model systematically underestimates reversal by 0.71 — there is no optimiser's curse to correct. Withdrawn: that figure was mostly a scale error.

A prediction made through the learned readout was being subtracted from a value on the simulator's own scale. Measured against the observed outcome through the same readout, the gap is −0.21. The underestimation is real but a third the size.

04
Both sensitivity readouts are unreliable — the oracle ranks below the models. Withdrawn: the diagnostic was broken, not the readouts.

The oracle exposed only a mean shift, so its "predicted population" was the control cells plus a constant — which cannot represent a population moving into a different basin. A diagnostic that condemns everything is usually the thing at fault.

05
No model beats the shuffled-label control on gene-holdout splits. Corrected: inconclusive at that size.

The first test compared confidence intervals for overlap, which is crude and conservative. Paired at the seed level the point estimates favour the models (+0.05 to +0.16). With 5 and 10 test perturbations per run the intervals simply cannot resolve it. Absence of evidence is not evidence of absence.

06
Deep models do not reliably beat linear baselines (supported). Downgraded to inconclusive.

The verdict flips depending on how "reliably beats" is defined, and the definition that produced it was written after seeing the win/loss counts. An audit script recomputes every verdict under both the original and revised rules.

VII
Plate VII · the search

Why the intervention search could not be tested

The design was to compare uncertainty-blind search against search that penalises uncertainty, expecting the penalty to help. It cannot be tested here: without any penalty the search already finds — of the best possible intervention. There is no room left for a penalty to recover, so it can only do harm, and at the largest penalty it does.

This is a flaw in the experiment, not a result about the idea

A design where the baseline already succeeds cannot discriminate between methods. Testing it properly needs a harder search problem, a larger candidate space or a weaker model, and that regime should be specified in writing before it is run, rather than built until the hypothesis appears.

VIII
Plate VIII · limitations

What this is not

No wet-lab validation. No biological or clinical claim.

Every number on this page comes from a simulator written for this project. Its structure encodes a hypothesis about how resistance states behave; it is not a measurement of one. Nothing here has been tested in cells, and the object of study is a predicted shift toward a drug-sensitive transcriptional state: not a treatment, not a cure, and not a demonstration that resistance can be reversed in anything living.

Further limits worth stating plainly: the sensitivity score is a learned proxy whose two independent readouts disagree sharply on predicted post-perturbation states. Only five seeds were run, which cannot reach conventional significance under the paired test used, so results are reported as intervals and effect sizes. And several analysis rules were revised after results arrived, logged with their counterfactual verdicts rather than presented as a clean story.

Reproducing it

Fixed seeds throughout. Every results file records the git commit, config and library versions, and carries a real or synthetic provenance tag. The full profile takes about nine hours on CPU.
