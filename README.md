# Reversing resistance in silico

Uncertainty-aware counterfactual search for interventions predicted to shift a
drug-resistant cell state toward a drug-sensitive transcriptional state.

Instead of modelling how to kill cancer cells, this project learns how perturbations move a
cell's transcriptional state, then searches for small interventions (at most `k = 3` gene
knockdowns) predicted to move a resistant cell toward a sensitive state — while calibrating
when the model should not be trusted and abstaining when it should not.

## What this is not

Everything here is **in silico**, on a **synthetic simulator**. There is no wet-lab
validation, and the only data used so far is generated by the code in this repository.

The object of study is a *predicted shift toward a drug-sensitive transcriptional state*.
That is not a treatment, not a cure, and not a demonstration that resistance can be reversed
in a patient or even in a cell line. The sensitivity function is a **learned proxy**, and on
synthetic data its two independent readouts already disagree sharply about whether a known
true reversal happened (see Limitations). Read `DATA.md` before interpreting any number.

## Method

- **Latent state** `z`: HVG selection, standardisation and PCA, all fitted on training cells
  only. Linear, so the decoder is exact and gene-space deltas are recoverable.
- **Response model** `f(z0, S)`: *PertFlow*, conditional flow matching on the marginal
  velocity field `v(z_t, t | S, context)`, with a DeepSets encoder over per-gene
  co-expression profiles so unseen combinations and unseen genes are representable. Deep
  ensemble of `M = 5`.
- **Sensitivity functional** `s(z)` in [0,1]: fitted on a **disjoint** experiment
  (unperturbed sensitive vs resistant cells, separate simulation seed). Two independent
  readouts — a latent classifier and a low-capacity expression signature — reported
  separately, never averaged.
- **Reversal score** `R(z0, S) = E[s(z')] - s(z0)`, plus energy distance from the predicted
  population to the sensitive-state distribution.
- **Uncertainty** `U(S)`: deep-ensemble disagreement on the predicted mean shift, turned
  into intervals by split conformal with the normalised score `|y - yhat| / spread`,
  calibrated on **held-out perturbations**.
- **Search**: `argmax E[R] - lambda * U(S)` over sets of size <= k by greedy, beam and
  evolutionary search, with exhaustive search as a reference so that search error and model
  error can be told apart. Abstains when `U` exceeds a calibrated threshold.

## Hypotheses

Each gets a verdict read from the numbers, including a negative one. `H1` is *expected* to
come out partly negative.

- **H1** Deep models do not reliably beat strong linear baselines under unseen-perturbation
  splits, and reversal-aware evaluation reorders the models relative to Pearson-delta.
- **H2** Ensemble plus split conformal reaches target coverage on unseen perturbations, and
  abstention lowers recommendation error.
- **H3** Uncertainty-penalised search gives higher held-out-verified reversal than
  uncertainty-blind search (the optimiser's curse).
- **H4** Active selection reaches a given accuracy with fewer experiments than random. *(not
  yet run)*
- **H5** Top-ranked targets are enriched for known resistance biology. *(not yet run; needs
  real data)*

## Results

All synthetic, 5 seeds. Full tables in `results/full/`, figures in `figures/full/`, and the
reasoning behind each verdict in `LOG.md`.

**H1 — deep models do not reliably beat linear baselines. Supported.**
4 decisive wins against 3 decisive losses over 20 paired comparisons, and the asymmetry is
the point: largest deep advantage **+0.097**, largest deep deficit **−0.605**.

The sharpest result is an ablation. PertFlow scores **0.229** Pearson-delta on unseen cell
context where ridge reaches 0.834 — but its own MLP variant, differing *only* in whether
prediction integrates an ODE, scores 0.830. The flow applies its context conditioning at
all 20 integration steps, so an untrained context embedding compounds; the regressor applies
it once. Iterative inference amplifies conditioning error under distribution shift.

The `shuffled_labels` control reaches 0.507 on unseen context and 0.557 on the composition
split, so roughly half the Pearson-delta signal does not require knowing which perturbation
was applied.

**H1b — reversal-aware evaluation reorders the models. Supported.**
5 of 8 comparisons change which model ranks first.

**H2 — coverage reached where exchangeability holds, and fails where it does not. Supported.**

| split | exchangeable | coverage (nominal 0.90) | uncertainty AUROC | abstention helps |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| combo (genes seen), n_cal 144 | yes | **0.888** [0.860, 0.914] | 0.697 | yes |
| combo (genes unseen), n_cal 30 | yes | 0.953 | 0.748 | yes |
| unseen single, n_cal 15 | yes | 1.000 (conservative) | 0.709 | yes |
| **unseen context** | **no** | **0.696** [0.660, 0.729] | **0.507** | **no** |

Under context shift three things fail together: coverage collapses, uncertainty becomes
uninformative (AUROC 0.507 is chance), and abstention stops helping. The shift that breaks
exchangeability also breaks the ensemble disagreement the calibration is built on.

**H3 — untestable in this regime. No verdict.**
At λ=0 the search already attains **99.94%** of the oracle optimum (regret 0.0006), so no
penalty has anything to recover; λ can only do harm, and at λ=2 it does. There is also no
optimiser's curse present to correct: the optimism gap is **−0.707**, i.e. the model
*under*estimates reversal (0.244 predicted vs 0.951 verified), while the curse requires
overestimation. Testing H3 needs a harder search problem. This is reported as untestable
rather than refuted.

**H4, H5** — not run.

### A statistical correction worth knowing about

The first version of these comparisons paired on `(seed, perturbation, context)`, treating
720 correlated rows as independent when there are only 5 fitted models. It produced tests
that contradicted their own confidence intervals (`mean_diff 0.0060, CI [−0.0053, 0.0179],
p = 0.0000`). Pairing is now at the seed level. A consequence: **with 5 seeds the Wilcoxon
signed-rank test cannot return p < 0.0625**, so significance at p < 0.05 is unreachable by
construction and differences are judged by interval and effect size instead.

## Running it

```bash
pip install -r requirements.txt
make test        # test suite
make smoke       # whole pipeline on a small profile, CPU, ~15 min measured
make reproduce   # full profile; regenerates every table and figure
```

There is **no GPU** on the development machine, so the full profile is sized for CPU rather
than assuming a device. Jobs over about ten minutes run detached with a log (`make h1`,
`make h2`, `make h3`).

## Reproducibility

Fixed seeds throughout; at least 5 seeds for stochastic models in the full profile. Every
results file records the config path, the git commit, and the Python, numpy and torch
versions. Splits are defined and written to disk before anything is fitted.

Leakage discipline is enforced by tests, not by care: HVG selection, scaling, PCA and the
gene embeddings are fitted on training cells only, calibration is grouped by perturbation,
and a held-out gene may not appear inside a training perturbation of any size. One test
deliberately constructs a leaked split and asserts that the checker rejects it, so the other
leakage tests cannot pass vacuously.

## Limitations

- **No wet-lab validation.** Nothing here has been tested in cells.
- **Synthetic data only.** The simulator was built for this project; its structure is a
  hypothesis about how resistance states behave, not a measurement of one. Any conclusion
  could be an artefact of it — `LOG.md` records three separate occasions where a plausible
  result turned out to be exactly that.
- **The sensitivity function is a proxy, and the two readouts disagree.** On a known true
  reversal (true sensitivity 0.10 to 0.98), the signature readout reported 0.21 to 0.95
  while the latent classifier reported 0.11 to 0.23. The headline reversal number depends on
  which proxy is chosen. Both are always reported.
- **Predicted effect magnitudes are shrunk** to roughly 0.6x of true on unseen
  perturbations. This is conditional-mean shrinkage rather than a fixable bug, and it biases
  reversal estimates toward zero (conservative) while largely preserving ranking.
- **Conformal exchangeability is violated under cell-context shift** by construction, so
  coverage on that split is reported separately and is not counted toward H2.
- **Gene-holdout splits are small**, bounded by the number of perturbable genes. Splits with
  fewer than five test perturbations are excluded from rank-correlation verdicts.
- **No real pharmacogenomic readout, no DepMap comparison, no pathway enrichment** yet.
